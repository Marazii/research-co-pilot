# Methodology (synthetic excerpt for example)

Mixed-methods study of remote-work mentorship. N=247 survey respondents (Aug 2024 panel) + 18 semi-structured interviews. Quant: regressed 12-month retention on mentorship-quality scale (Eby et al. 2008, α=.84), controlling for tenure, salary band, and prior network density. Qual: reflexive thematic analysis (Braun & Clarke) of interviews; 5 themes after iteration.

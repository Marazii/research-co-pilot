## Decision

**Approve with required revisions** — 4 required revisions before submission, 3 recommended changes, 2 notes for consideration.

## Required revisions

1. **Recruitment via Facebook professional groups raises group-level consent issues.** The groups exist for professional discussion, not for research recruitment. Required: explicit permission from group admins; recruitment post that clearly identifies you, the study, the IRB, and the option not to participate. Do not scrape the group for direct messaging.
2. **Re-identifiability risk in "nurses + workplace stigma" combination.** A small-N subgroup analysis (e.g., a specific specialty in a specific region) could be re-identifiable. Required: minimum cell-size threshold (n≥10) for any subgroup reporting in the paper; aggregate to broader categories below threshold.
3. **Consent form documentation absent.** Required: full consent form (purpose, procedures, risks, benefits, voluntariness, withdrawal, confidentiality, IRB contact, your contact) presented before survey items. Attention-check item recommended.
4. **Data management plan absent.** Required: where data is stored (encrypted), who has access, retention period, destruction plan, sharing decisions. Survey platform's data-handling terms must be reviewed and disclosed.

## Recommended changes

1. **$5 compensation:** reasonable, non-coercive. Consider gift-card-vs-cash equivalence for any participants whose institutions may restrict gift acceptance.
2. **Optional Certificate of Confidentiality** for sensitive disclosures (workplace stigma can have employment consequences if breached). NIH CoC available for research on sensitive topics.
3. **Pilot the survey** with 5-10 nurses for cognitive interviews before fielding. Burden estimate, item comprehension, sensitive-item flow.

## Notes for consideration

1. The "professional Facebook group" recruitment biases sample toward digitally-active members of professional networks. Note in limitations.
2. Anonymous survey or coded? If coded for follow-up, the coding key must be separately stored with described access control.
